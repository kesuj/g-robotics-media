A drone sensor that transmits nothing? Yes, and it's real. 📡
FIU engineers detect small drones from the magnetic field their motors leak: loop antenna, neural network, a few hundred dollars to develop.
The catch: about 15 m range outdoors, 98.5% detection. Models were harder to tell apart.
