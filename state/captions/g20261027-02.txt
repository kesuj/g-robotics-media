Before the Moon, a rehearsal in Earth orbit. 🚀
NASA's Artemis III (planned 2027) will dock Orion with test versions of Blue Origin's Blue Moon and SpaceX's Starship. No landing yet — a crewed landing is the 2028 target.
Why it matters: docking and cabin checks are cheaper to fix in orbit than at the Moon.

Sources: nasa.gov
