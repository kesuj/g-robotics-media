Fiber made in space 🛰️
About 11.8 km of ZBLAN fiber was drawn on the ISS. Microgravity cuts crystal defects, but expected gains are ~2x at best, not the 100x in theory — impurities are the bottleneck. Early results; the ISS retires in 2030.

Sources: nasa.gov, spectrum.ieee.org
