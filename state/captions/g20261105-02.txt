Drones were banned from this cell band for 35 years. Now the US is lifting it. 📡
The FCC voted to open 50 MHz of the 800 MHz cellular band to drones. The 1991 rule feared one airborne phone could hit many towers at once; the FCC now says modern networks manage that interference.
The catch: no conditions or start date reported yet, and it's a US rule only.
