A drone battery swap in 25 seconds. That's DJI Dock 4. 🚁
The drone-in-a-box dock changes batteries by itself; other docks typically need 90 seconds to 2 minutes. It launched with the Matrice 5TD, rated up to 59 minutes of flight in DJI's tests.
The catch: no price announced, and it's unlikely to be sold in the US.
