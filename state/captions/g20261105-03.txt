Google put an AI chip in orbit. Its first job: write a haiku. 🛰️
The Suncatcher prototype, built with Planet Labs, carries four TPUs but runs one at a time, about 20 minutes per run. It's testing Gemma 4 while engineers check radiation and heat effects.
The catch: this is a long-term moonshot. Two more satellites are planned for 2027.
