Myth: space makes perfect optical fibre. 🛰️
Fact: ZBLAN glass forms fewer crystals in orbit, and ~11.9 km was drawn on the ISS — but the best loss cut so far is ~2×, versus ~100× in theory. Impurities and cost remain the limits.

Sources: nasa.gov, spectrum.ieee.org
