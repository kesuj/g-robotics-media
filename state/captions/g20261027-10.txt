A Mars rover just took the off-world driving record. 🔴
On 11 Oct 2026 NASA's Perseverance passed Opportunity's ~45.16 km, reaching ~45.22 km. IEEE Spectrum credits AutoNav self-driving for most of it — but humans still plan routes and mark no-go sand.
Why care: autonomy is what makes distance possible when radio delay rules out a joystick.

Sources: guinnessworldrecords.com, spectrum.ieee.org
